After completing my MFA and producing Map of Brooklyn (2020), I have been awarded a prestigious Full Research Scholarship to Adelaide University in Australia. This is not just a degree; it is a significant international investment in South African storytelling.

The Vision: Interdisciplinary Research

My work interrogates post-apartheid cultural identity, negotiating trauma, memory, and belonging. It is a practice-led exploration that sits at the intersection of traditional forms and new technologies.

  • ‣ Hybrid Narrative: Blending creative writing with transmedial forms.
  • ‣ Decolonial Aesthetics: Re-framing storytelling through a South African lens.
  • ‣ AI & Tech: Exploring ethical dimensions of representation in the age of AI.
